Multi-Occupant Stage Rank

The Multi-Occupant Vehicle Class Stage Ranking is based upon a scoring formula. 50% of the score is distance completed and 50% is an “Optimization Score”. The Optimization score is calculated based on the Person Miles completed per External kWh Charged during the event. The team with the best score in each component gets 50pts, for a highest possible score of 100pts. The scores in each category are scaled according to the highest one.

Scrutineering

Before they can hit the track, solar cars go through a series of technical inspections called Scrutineering. They must pass a series of static inspections followed by dynamic tests.

Safety

Verifies teams have essential safety equipment, including fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and emergency gear.

Driver Registration

Confirms drivers (and MOV passengers) have valid licenses, approved helmets, safe footwear, and the required ballast weight.

Driver Operations

Ensures drivers fit in the car, have necessary onboard equipment (water, radio, ballast holder), and can exit the vehicle within 10 seconds.

Lights & Vision

Checks the driver’s field of view, rear vision capabilities, and compliance with brightness and visibility requirements for vehicle lights.

Body & Sizing

Confirms vehicle dimensions, solar array size, seat belt placement, signage, headrests, and other structural regulations.

Electrical

Reviews battery size, driver controls, emergency stop functionality, and overall compliance with electrical safety and competition rules.

Battery Protection

Tests battery systems to ensure automatic shutoff in unsafe voltage, current, or temperature conditions.

Mechanical

Inspects the vehicle’s body, chassis, roll cage, brakes, steering, and suspension for adherence to safety and performance standards.

Dynamics

Requires vehicles to pass U-turn, figure-eight, slalom, and braking tests, with teams achieving at least yellow status in prior stations before attempting.

Array Testing

Verifies the performance and compliance of the vehicle’s solar array.

Impound & MOV

Demonstrates proper battery impoundment outside event hours; Multi-Occupant Vehicles undergo additional metering verification for external charging.

ASC Support

Support vehicles are checked for proper lights, signage, safety equipmentt, and communication with the solar car.
